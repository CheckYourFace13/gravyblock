# GravyBlock video library (plan)

GravyBlock should end up with many purposeful videos, not one generic trailer. Each answers one question and goes through the unlisted review, email and approval flow.

- What GravyBlock does
- The autonomous growth loop
- Business Truth
- SEO, search and local growth
- Marketing Studio
- Reporting and proof
- Onboarding
- Pricing and value
- FAQs and objections
- Feature-specific demos
- Proof and results clips

Use: the iScream Studio YouTube channel, and embedded where useful on gravyblock.com.

Marketing Studio may create, publish (through the review flow) and measure these. It may NOT modify gravyblock.com: House Accounts are read-only toward their sites, enforced by `scripts/ops/check-marketing-isolation.mjs`. Placing a video on the site is normal intentional development.
